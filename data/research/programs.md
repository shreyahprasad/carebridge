# CareBridge research: DC assistance and transportation programs

Checked 2026-11-06. The full records, with verbatim excerpts, are in `programs.json`. There are 23 programs: assistance 9, transportation 8, crisis 3, rights 3. Confidence: high means an official page states it; medium means the source is official but dated or indirect; low means a secondary source.

**Big picture:** Since Oct 1, 2026, adult refugees and asylees who do not have a green card have lost full DC Medicaid. Pregnant people and children under 21 who are lawfully residing are exempt. Since Oct 1, 2025, the DC Health Care Alliance takes no new adults aged 26 or older, and its income limit is 138% FPL.

## How this applies to case 1 (pregnant adult, Dari/Farsi, recently arrived, has Medicaid)

- **Her status decides her coverage:**
  - *Refugee or asylee:* she stays on full Medicaid, because pregnant people are exempt from the Oct 2026 cut. This lasts through 12 months postpartum. After that, as an adult refugee or asylee without a green card, she would likely lose Medicaid; check her LPR status.
  - *Undocumented:* her pregnancy coverage is CHIP FCEP, with only 2 months postpartum. Emergency Medicaid applies after that. The Alliance takes no new adults aged 26+, so her age matters.
  - *Asylum applicant (pending):* whether she counts as "lawfully residing" is not confirmed. FCEP covers her regardless.
- **Rides:** Find out whether she is FFS or managed care. Pregnancy coverage (Medicaid and FCEP) runs through managed care, so she most likely calls AmeriHealth (1-800-315-3485, ≥48 h ahead, no Sundays) or MedStar (866-201-9974, 24/7). FFS members call MTM at 1-866-796-0601, ≥3 business days ahead. If her household gets SNAP, Metro Lift halves her fares.
- **Interpreter:** Book a Dari interpreter for in-person visits; for FFS Medicaid that is Context Global, ≥3 days ahead. DC agencies must interpret under the Language Access Act.
- **Mental health:** Medicaid covers confidential behavioral health. The DBH Access HelpLine (1-888-793-4357) connects her to in-person DBH care. AmeriHealth's Bright Start program helps pregnant members with rides and postpartum behavioral health.
- **Woman provider:** No program page we read lets her filter for a woman provider. The caseworker has to ask the plan or provider directly.

## How this applies to case 2 (17-year-old, Spanish-speaking, status undocumented or pending)

- **Coverage:** If she is undocumented or has a pending case, she can get Health Care Alliance for Children (under 21, ≤319% FPL, mental health through DBH). If she is lawfully residing and under 21, she keeps Medicaid; the Oct 2026 cut does not apply to children.
- **Consent:** Under D.C. Code § 7-1231.14 she can get outpatient therapy without a parent if she seeks it voluntarily and it is clinically indicated. This lasts 90 days at a time, and her parents are told only with her consent. Medication needs a parent, except at 16+ in the listed cases.
- **Transport:**
  - If she attends a DC school, Kids Ride Free gives her free bus and rail within DC. The program is for school trips; medical trips are not addressed.
  - If she is on Medicaid, she can use her plan's rides.
  - **The Alliance does not provide transport.** As an Alliance member she would need Kids Ride Free or Metro Lift (if her family gets SNAP).
- **Crisis:** 988 text and chat are available in Spanish. The DBH Access HelpLine, available 24/7, can send a mobile crisis team for children.
- **If she becomes pregnant:** she can get FCEP regardless of status.
